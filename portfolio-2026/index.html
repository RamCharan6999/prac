DEBUGGING MINDSET

If it only works in a controlled demo, I don't consider it finished.

Observe

Reproduce the failure and capture enough evidence to stop guessing.

Isolate

Sensor → transport → state → decision → actuation. Find the broken boundary.

Validate

Fix the cause, then test across conditions that should have broken it again.

FEATURED SYSTEM · RESEARCH + REAL VEHICLE

Radar-guided camera verification for Automatic Emergency Braking.

Read the preprint ↗

Instead of running vision over the entire frame, radar tells the camera where to look. A lightweight camera confirmation step validates the target before braking — no model weights, no training dataset, no GPU dependency.

WHY THE ROI MATTERS

Less vision compute, same safety question.

Mean per-detection processing fell from 0.706 ms for the full frame to 0.127 ms for the radar-guided ROI — about 5.5× faster in the evaluated dataset.
